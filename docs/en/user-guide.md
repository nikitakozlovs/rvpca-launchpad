# RVP CA launchpad — user guide

The launchpad is a single page collecting the internal webapps of the Central
Administration of the City of Rīga municipality (Rīgas valstspilsētas pašvaldības
Centrālā administrācija). From here you can reach every system you have access to,
without hunting through bookmarks.

Latviešu valodā: [`docs/lv/lietotaja-rokasgramata.md`](../lv/lietotaja-rokasgramata.md)

> The launchpad interface itself is in Latvian only. This guide is the English
> translation of the Latvian user documentation.

---

## How to read the launchpad

Apps are arranged in **groups by function** — for example *Iepirkumi un finanses*
(Procurement and finance), *Cilvēki* (People), *Pārvaldība un rīki* (Governance
and tools). Each group shows a number next to its title: how many apps it holds.

Tiles are deliberately different sizes: the more widely used systems take more
room. Size changes nothing about how an app behaves — it is ordering only.

Each tile carries:

- **A mark** — the product's stacked-square monogram, or a two-letter monogram
  for apps that do not have their own artwork drawn yet.
- **A title** — the system's name.
- **A description** — one line on what the system does.

## Opening an app

Click the tile. The app opens in the same tab.

To open it alongside, use middle-click or `Ctrl` + click (`Cmd` + click on Mac) —
tiles are real links, so your browser's usual habits work.

## What "Izstrādē" means

A tile with a grey dashed border and an **`IZSTRĀDĒ`** ("in development") badge is
a system being built that is not available yet. It is shown so you know what is
coming, but it cannot be clicked — there is no link behind it.

These tiles are also muted compared to the rest: the mark uses its single-colour
version and the text is quietened.

When the system becomes available, the tile turns into an ordinary, launchable
tile.

## Keyboard

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `Tab` | Move between tiles |
| `Enter` | Open the focused tile |

## Light and dark

At the right of the top bar is a moon / sun button. It switches the launchpad
between light (paper) and dark (Midsummer-night blue).

Your choice is remembered in this browser, so the launchpad opens the way you
left it.

## On phone and tablet

The launchpad adapts to screen width. On a phone the tiles stack into one column.
All the content is the same — nothing is hidden away.

## If something is missing

- **An app is not listed.** The launchpad's contents are maintained by the
  development team. Ask them and the system will be added.
- **An app is listed but will not open.** You most likely lack access rights.
  Access is granted by each system's owner, not by the launchpad.
- **A tile is marked `IZSTRĀDĒ`.** The system is not ready yet. Wait for the
  development team's announcement.
